We are looking for highly motivated, enthusiastic members interested in (1) improving plant traits and soil health through plant-soil-microbe interactions, and (2) unraveling the water, nutrient, and carbon cycles that connect agricultural landscapes to productivity and environmental sustainability.

<div class="card-side-list" style="margin-top:1.6em;">

  <div class="card theme-card crop card-float">
    <span class="tag">Area 1</span>
    <h3>Plant-soil &amp; plant-soil-microbe interactions</h3>
    <button type="button" class="card-float-img" data-lightbox-src="{{ '/assets/img/research/areas/area-1-plant-soil-microbe.jpg' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Enlarge Plant-soil-microbe interaction illustration">
      <img src="{{ '/assets/img/research/areas/area-1-plant-soil-microbe.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Plant-soil-microbe interaction illustration">
    </button>
    <p>This position is to build multi-scale plant-soil and plant-soil-microbe interaction models and further integrate these models with multi-omics and meta-omics data to investigate the synergies and tradeoffs of multiple plant or/and microbial traits and their impacts on crop water use efficiency (WUE) and nitrogen use efficiency (NUE). The work is expected to be fully integrated with advanced breeding and bioengineering programs. Candidates with strong research interests, background, and potential in computational systems biology, quantitative genetics, crop growth modeling, plant functional-structural modeling, root growth and rhizosphere modeling, soil hydrology and reactive transport modeling, microbial ecology and biogeochemistry modeling, and earth system modeling are encouraged to apply.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="card theme-card water card-float">
    <span class="tag water">Area 2</span>
    <h3>Field-to-watershed hydrology &amp; water quality</h3>
    <button type="button" class="card-float-img" data-lightbox-src="{{ '/assets/img/research/areas/area-2-field-to-watershed.jpg' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Enlarge Field-to-watershed hydrology and water quality illustration">
      <img src="{{ '/assets/img/research/areas/area-2-field-to-watershed.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Field-to-watershed hydrology and water quality illustration">
    </button>
    <p>From the monitoring side, this position is to discover fundamental knowledge about water and nutrient cycling with high-frequency hydrological and water quality monitoring data from IoT sensor networks and to reveal spatial patterns of hydrological and water quality variables through integrated sensing with unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) and satellites. From the modeling side, this position is to build next-generation field-to-watershed hydrological and water quality models and use systems modeling to advance our understanding of the complex scaling from field-scale conservation management to watershed-scale hydrological and water quality responses. Candidates with strong research interests, background, and potential in sensor networks, remote sensing (UAV and satellite), agroecosystem modeling, biogeochemical modeling, coupled surface-subsurface hydrological modeling, watershed hydrological modeling, reactive transport modeling, and river hydraulic modeling are particularly encouraged to apply.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="card theme-card clay card-float">
    <span class="tag clay">Area 3</span>
    <h3>AI for science &amp; environmental data science</h3>
    <button type="button" class="card-float-img" data-lightbox-src="{{ '/assets/img/research/areas/area-3-ai-data-science.jpg' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Enlarge AI for science and environmental data science illustration">
      <img src="{{ '/assets/img/research/areas/area-3-ai-data-science.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="AI for science and environmental data science illustration">
    </button>
    <p>This position is to leverage state-of-the-art AI and machine learning to advance research in crop, soil, and environmental sciences. Topics of interest include LLM-based multi-agent systems for scientific reasoning, reinforcement learning for decision making, knowledge-guided machine learning and differentiable models for prediction problems in crop, soil, and environmental sciences, and deep learning in remote sensing and geospatial big data analytics. Candidates with strong research interests, background, and potential in LLM, AI, machine learning, deep learning, quantitative remote sensing (optical, thermal, and microwave), and geospatial analytics are particularly encouraged to apply. Experience in handling large-scale datasets is highly preferred.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="card theme-card gold card-float">
    <span class="tag gold">Area 4</span>
    <h3>Agriculture in the Earth system &amp; sustainable agrifood systems</h3>
    <button type="button" class="card-float-img" data-lightbox-src="{{ '/assets/img/research/areas/area-4-earth-system.jpg' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Enlarge Agriculture in the Earth system illustration">
      <img src="{{ '/assets/img/research/areas/area-4-earth-system.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Agriculture in the Earth system illustration">
    </button>
    <p>This position is to improve the representation of agriculture and human management in Earth System Models for assessing different land management strategies and quantifying water (blue, green, and gray) and carbon footprints of agricultural production at regional to global scales. Candidates with strong research interests, background, and potential in earth system modeling, large-scale hydrological modeling, ecosystem modeling, crop growth modeling, and geospatial data science are encouraged to apply.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="card theme-card water card-float">

Candidates with strong research interests, background, and potential in geospatial data science, process-based and data-driven modeling, decision support systems, and real-world solutions are encouraged to apply.</p>
