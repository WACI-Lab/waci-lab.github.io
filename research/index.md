Our work sits at the intersection of hydrology, soil and crop sciences, remote sensing, and artificial intelligence. We combine field and lab measurement, process-based and computational modeling, satellite and UAV sensing, and AI to understand how water, nutrients, and carbon flow across agricultural landscapes, and how those flows shape food security, water quality, and agroecosystem resilience.
