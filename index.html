Water, Agriculture, and Conservation Innovation Lab · UIUC

Tracing water, nutrients, and carbon through working landscapes.

The WACI Lab studies how water, nutrient, and carbon cycles shape agricultural productivity and environmental health — from a single root to a crop field, to an entire watershed, and to the whole Earth system — using field and lab measurements, computational modeling, remote sensing, and artificial intelligence.
