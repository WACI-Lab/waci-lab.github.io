<p>The Water and Environmental Test or WETest Lab is part of WACI, offering analytical chemistry services that support water and environmental research &mdash; including agricultural water management, surface water (streams, ponds, and lakes) and groundwater quality assessment. Beyond our own research, we serve University of Illinois researchers as well as customers outside the University.</p>
<p>The lab is directed by {{ site.data.team.pi.name }}, with day-to-day operations led by Michael P. Wallace, Research Specialist and Head Lab Technician.</p>
