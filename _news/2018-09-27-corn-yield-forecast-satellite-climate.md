As lead author, Dr. Peng developed a corn-yield forecasting system that combines NOAA seasonal climate predictions with satellite imagery, run on the Blue Waters supercomputer. The approach beat the USDA's WASDE estimates well before harvest &mdash; June predictions were off by 12.75 bushels per acre, compared to 17.66 for WASDE. Read more at [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2018-09-accurately-end-of-season-corn-yield.html).
