Dr. Peng co-authored a new framework that reconciles several competing theories of how plants regulate transpiration under water stress — plant hydraulics, supply-demand balance, and empirical soil-water-stress functions — into one consistent approach, published in the *Journal of Advances in Modeling Earth Systems*.
