Dr. Peng co-developed the model-data fusion approach, integrating the ecosys agroecosystem model with satellite observations, USDA statistics, and eddy covariance flux tower measurements to more accurately quantify carbon cycling in Midwestern croplands &mdash; supporting the SMARTFARM Project's goal of field-scale carbon credit quantification. Read more at [iSEE](https://isee.illinois.edu/new-modeling-solution-sets-the-bar-for-quantifying-carbon-budget-and-credit-in-agroecosystems/).
