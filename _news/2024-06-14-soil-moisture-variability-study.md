Dr. Peng led a multi-institutional study showing that the spatial pattern of soil moisture variability within a field stays consistent across growing and non-growing seasons — a finding that opens the door to using satellite-based optical remote sensing for future soil moisture monitoring, reducing reliance on costly field sensors. Read more at [ASC](https://asc.illinois.edu/uncategorized/asc-led-study-reveals-stable-soil-moisture-variability-within-fields-and-opens-the-door-for-satellite-remote-sensing-for-future-measurements/).
