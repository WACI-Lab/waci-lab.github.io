Dr. Peng led this Nature Plants study, with Kaiyu Guan, arguing that crop models need to integrate processes across scales &mdash; from plant genetics and physiology up to regional and global assessments &mdash; to reliably evaluate climate change adaptation strategies for crop and field management. Read more at [Illinois News Bureau](https://news.illinois.edu/study-multiscale-crop-modeling-effort-required-to-assess-climate-change-adaptation/).
