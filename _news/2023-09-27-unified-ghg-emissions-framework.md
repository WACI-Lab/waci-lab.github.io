Dr. Peng co-authored this framework integrating multiple sensing technologies, satellite imagery, and AI-based ecosystem modeling into a &ldquo;system of systems&rdquo; solution for measuring how climate mitigation practices &mdash; such as cover cropping and precision fertilizer management &mdash; reduce greenhouse gas emissions at the individual field level, generic and scalable enough to apply to agricultural systems in any country. Read more at [Illinois News Bureau](https://news.illinois.edu/researchers-propose-a-unified-scalable-framework-to-measure-agricultural-greenhouse-gas-emissions/).
