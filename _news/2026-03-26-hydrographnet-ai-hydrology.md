Dr. Peng and Dr. Jie Yang from WACI led a study introducing HydroGraphNet, a knowledge-guided AI model that represents a watershed as a connected network of sub-basins to improve daily predictions of streamflow and nitrogen export, tested in the Upper Sangamon River Basin. The paper appears in *Water Research*. Read more at [ASC](https://asc.illinois.edu/uncategorized/ai-meets-hydrology-illinois-scientists-develop-new-system-to-track-nitrogen-pollution-acrosswatersheds/).
