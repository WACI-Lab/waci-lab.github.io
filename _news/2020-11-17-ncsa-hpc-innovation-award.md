NCSA received a 2020 HPC Innovation Excellence Award recognizing the CLM-APSIM crop forecasting model, which Dr. Peng co-developed with Kaiyu Guan and Jian Peng on the Blue Waters supercomputer &mdash; combining satellite data, earth system modeling, and machine learning into one of the most reliable long-term corn-yield prediction tools for the U.S. Corn Belt. Read more at [NCSA](https://www.ncsa.illinois.edu/2020/11/17/ncsa-receives-honors-in-2020-hpc-innovation-excellence-awards/).
