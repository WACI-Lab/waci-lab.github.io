Our new paper, led by Zewei Ma with co-corresponding authorship from Dr. Peng and Dr. Guan, examines how soil oxygen dynamics mediate the effects of tile drainage on hydrology, biogeochemistry, and crop productivity. Read it in [Hydrology and Earth System Sciences](https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-29-6393-2025).
